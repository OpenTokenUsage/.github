# OpenTokenUsage

Every AI coding limit, next to your clock. A free, open-source tray app for **Windows 11**.

OpenTokenUsage shows how much of your AI coding subscriptions you've used — session and weekly limits, credits, reset times and pace — for Claude, Codex, Cursor, Copilot, Antigravity and a dozen more providers. Your data stays on your PC: no account, no telemetry.

```powershell
winget install PowerUserZ.OpenTokenUsage
```
